## Tips

- **Personalize the opening** — reference something specific from their profile. Generic DMs get ignored.
- **Do not promise jobs** — promise the program. Let the program speak.
- **Send the GitHub link, not a job form** — serious people will read the architecture. Unserious people will self-select out.
- **Follow up once** if no response in 2 weeks. Not more.
- **Track who you've sent this to** — keep a simple spreadsheet to avoid double-messaging.
